Frequently asked questions

What is the minimum group size for a private event at Schnock?

From 10 guests at the counter table. The Salon Cheminée seats 24 (30 standing), the Salle Beaulieu seats 25 (30 standing) and the Salle comptoir holds 35 standing. The whole restaurant can be booked for up to 60 seated or 100 standing.

How much are the group menus?

Lunch: €32 (starter + main or main + dessert) or €38 (starter + main + dessert). Dinner: €55, or €80 for the prestige menu. After-work drinks at the bar: €25 per person. Prices per person, VAT included.

Are drinks included?

Menus include mineral water (1 bottle for 2) and hot drinks. You can add a welcome cocktail or glass of Champagne (€14) and a wine pairing chosen by our sommelier, from €17 per person.

Can you adapt the menu for allergies or vegetarian diets?

Yes. Every menu has a vegetarian option and we adapt dishes to your guests' allergies and diets. Just tell us in your quote request.

Which days are available for private events?

Private events take place at lunch and dinner, Monday to Friday. For a weekend date, get in touch: we look at every request.

How do I get to Schnock?

Schnock is at 31 rue Guillaume Tell, Paris 17th, a short walk from Place Pereire: Pereire metro (line 3), RER C Pereire-Levallois, Champerret car park. Valet parking is available.

How soon will I get a quote?

We reply within one working day with a proposal tailored to your event. You can also call us on +33 1 47 64 86 04.
